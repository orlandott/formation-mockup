Working to keep the future open

Formation Research studies lock-in risk: the danger that a harmful feature of the world — a dictatorship, a monopoly, an entrenched ideology — becomes stable for a very long time. We research how lock-in happens and develop interventions to reduce the risk.

The problem

What is lock-in?

Lock-in is where some feature of the world — technological, political, or institutional — is held stable for a long time. Possibly for centuries. Possibly forever.

Some lock-ins are harmless. QWERTY is still the standard keyboard layout not because it is the best design, but because switching away from it now costs too much. Others would be catastrophic: a totalitarian regime, or a monopoly over resources and labour, held in place indefinitely.

AI is the reason to take this seriously now. Software does not age, forget, or die. An AI system can preserve a goal — or a ruler's ideology — without the drift and succession problems that have brought past regimes to an end. That could make harmful arrangements far more durable than anything that has come before.

Focus areas

The scenarios we work to prevent

AI-enabled totalitarianism

A ruler could use AI systems to extend their own lifespan and run mass surveillance, producing a stable totalitarian regime with no natural end.

Extreme power concentration

The people at the forefront of AI could end up with enormous leverage over the technological and political direction of humanity, creating lasting monopolies over resources and labour.

Our approach

What we do about it

We work on two fronts: researching the fundamental dynamics of how lock-in happens, and building practical interventions that reduce the risk.

We start from first principles, constructing our own models from basic facts about AI systems and testing the assumptions before relying on them. We work alongside AI safety organisations and outside researchers rather than in isolation, and we publish what we find. What we are after is knowledge that can be applied to the systems that exist today, and we update the agenda as the evidence changes.

Current research focus

Secret loyalties

A secret loyalty is a hidden objective built into an AI model. A model with one favours a particular person or group, and the behaviour switches on only under conditions chosen by whoever put it there — so it can pass unnoticed in ordinary use.

Secret loyalties are one concrete route to AI-enabled authoritarianism and extreme power concentration. They can be studied, and defended against, in today's systems — which is what our technical work focuses on.
