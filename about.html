About us

Who we are

Formation Research Ltd is a UK-based not-for-profit company limited by guarantee, with 501(c)(3) equivalency through NGO Source, on a mission to understand how lock-in happens and reduce the risk.

A lock-in is a situation where some negative feature of human culture becomes stable for a long time. We focus on the worst scenarios, such as AI-enabled totalitarianism and extreme concentrations of power.

Focus

The scenarios we study

AI-enabled totalitarianism

A ruler could use AI to extend their lifespan and run mass surveillance, producing a regime with no natural end.

Extreme power concentration

The people at the forefront of AI could gain lasting leverage over humanity's direction, with monopolies over resources and labour.

Current research focus: secret loyalties

A secret loyalty is a hidden objective built into an AI model. It favours a particular person or group and switches on only under conditions its creator chose. Secret loyalties are a concrete mechanism for AI-enabled authoritarianism, and they can be studied and countered in today's systems. That is where our research effort currently goes.

Our approach

How we work

Scientific and technical investigation into AI systems, done in the open and with others.

From first principles

We build our own models from basic facts about AI systems and test assumptions before relying on them.

Scientific

We work by conjecture, peer review, and error-correction, and update the agenda as the evidence changes.

Collaborative

We work with AI safety organisations and researchers across disciplines.

Aimed at practice

We aim for knowledge that produces practical, real-world mitigations for lock-in risk.
